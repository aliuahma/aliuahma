I've compiled a list of advice I've received from others and learned myself. If it is useful to at least one of you, then I'm happy. In no particular order:

  1. There is too much variance in trying to predict long-term effects. Make locally optimal choices in the current moment, and trust that it will work out.
  2. It's better to be the best at one thing than mediocre in a lot of things.
