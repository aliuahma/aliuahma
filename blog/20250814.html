1030 lions or the Sun, who wins?

August 14, 2025

Big answers to even bigger questions.

Let's consider the energy involved. The mass of the Sun is approximately $M_{\odot} = 1.989 \times 10^{30}$ kg, while a lion has a mass of roughly $m_{\text{lion}} = 200$ kg.

So our army of $10^{30}$ lions has a total mass of: $$M_{\text{lions}} = 10^{30} \times 200 \text{ kg} = 2 \times 10^{32} \text{ kg}$$

This means our lion army outweighs the Sun by a factor of: $$\frac{M_{\text{lions}}}{M_{\odot}} = \frac{2 \times 10^{32}}{1.989 \times 10^{30}} \approx 100$$

But mass isn't everything. The Sun's luminosity is $L_{\odot} = 3.828 \times 10^{26}$ watts, which means it releases energy equivalent to: $$E = \frac{L_{\odot}}{c^2} \times c^2 = 4.26 \times 10^9 \text{ kg/s}$$ of mass-energy conversion per second.
