Maximal Update Parameterization (μP) has proven to be a powerful tool for the practice and study of scaling up neural networks . Despite this, μP remains an elusive concept to graspA sentiment shared by several experienced practitioners that I have spoken to. due to its relative complexity. In this blog, I attempt to provide a clear and practical overview of μP, in hopes that at least one more person may be able to appreciate its beauty and effectiveness. We will discuss the motivations for μP, the mathematical framework, and its practical implementation.

There are perhaps two ways to motivate the idea of μP: (1) it is desirable to be able to tune hyperparameters at a small scale and zero-shot transfer them to large scale, and (2) we want our theories of preventing activation explosion to persist in the infinite-width limit. You may consider the former to be an engineer's perspective, and the latter to be a physicist's perspective.
