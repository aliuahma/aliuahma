Resources for Generative Modeling

Home

I became interested in generative modeling in June of this year (2025). I still have a lot to learn, but I've compiled a list of resources and papers that I find/found very useful. I'll continue to add onto this as I read and learn more! Let me know if there's anything you think I should list.

Blogs

  • All of Sander Dieleman's blogs
  • Generative Modeling by Estimating Gradients of the Data Distribution, Song, Yang
  • Flow With What You Know, Hawley, Scott H.
  • Diffusion Meets Flow Matching: Two Sides of the Same Coin, Ruiqi Gao, et al.
  • Lilian Weng's blogs on generative models
  • Diffusion is not necessarily Spectral Autoregression, Fabian Falck

Papers (Disclaimer: these are some of my personal favorites. For a more comprehensive [yet still incomplete] list, click here.)

  • Elucidating the Design Space of Diffusion-Based Generative Models, Karras, et al. (Jun 2022)
  • Classifier-Free Diffusion Guidance, Ho and Salimans (Jul 2022)
  • Flow Straight and Fast: Learning to Generate and Transfer Data with Rectified Flow, Liu, et al. (Sep 2022)
  • Analyzing and Improving the Training Dynamics of Diffusion Models, Karras, et al. (Dec 2023)
  • Mean Flows for One-step Generative Modeling, Geng, et al. (May 2025)

Courses/Textbooks

  • MIT 6.S184: Flow Matching and Diffusion Models
