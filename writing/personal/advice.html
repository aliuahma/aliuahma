Advice for a Younger Me

October 10, 2025

Home

What follows is a list of advice I wish I had heard when I was younger, and things I think are generally useful. If it's useful to at least one person reading, then I'm happy. I will try to keep this list updated. In no particular order:

What's worth doing is worth doing well.

Your only limit is what you think you can't do.

Be delusional about things you can control, and pragmatic about the rest.

Document things for posterity.

Try to learn something from everyone you meet.

Don't give your time to those that won't respect it.

Trying to predict long-term outcomes is a futile endeavor; there's too much variance. Make locally optimal choices, and trust that it will work out.

It's better to be excellent at one thing than good at a lot of things.

Your health comes above everything else.

Your brain is a muscle, so do things that require you to think deeply.

Asking good questions is a skill, but you can improve by first determining what you want to learn from someone, and asking lots of questions.

Your brain compresses time when your life is monotonous, so expose yourself to new experiences often.

Be genuinely curious about people.

Surround yourself with people you can learn from. Learn from the best.

A week is 2% of the year. Make every second count, whether you're working effectively or relaxing effectively.

Ask for what you want, you'd be surprised how often you'll get it
